# Sample Q&A

**Q1: What is the expense ratio of SBI Bluechip Fund?**
**A:** The Direct Plan of SBI Bluechip Fund has an expense ratio of 0.85%, while the Regular Plan is 1.58%.
*Source: https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/factsheets/sbi-bluechip-fund*

**Q2: What is the lock-in period for the ELSS fund?**
**A:** The SBI Long Term Equity Fund (ELSS) has a mandatory statutory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of allotment.
*Source: https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/sbi-long-term-equity-fund-kim*

**Q3: What is the exit load for SBI Flexicap?**
**A:** 10% of the investment can be redeemed without exit load within 1 year. For the remaining investment, a 1% exit load applies if redeemed within 1 year. Nil after 1 year.
*Source: https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/sbi-flexicap-fund-kim*

**Q4: How do I download my capital gains statement?**
**A:** Log in to the SBI MF portal, navigate to the 'Statements' section, select 'Capital Gains Statement', choose the financial year, and click download.
*Source: https://www.sbimf.com/en-us/investor-services/capital-gains*
